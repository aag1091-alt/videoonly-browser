VideoOnly Browser

Type or paste a page address in the bar above. Popups, redirects to other sites and file downloads are blocked automatically; when a video starts playing, everything except the player is hidden.

Blocked items show up in the shield counter on the right; click it to open one on purpose. The gear menu switches ad-blocking, redirect blocking, auto video-only and the video-only style (player vs bare video). The arrow next to “Video only” has picture-in-picture and playback speed.
